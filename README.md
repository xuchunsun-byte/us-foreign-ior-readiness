# US Foreign IOR Readiness Snapshot (preview)

Unofficial comparison checklist for non-U.S. sellers shipping to the United States on DDP terms, or acting as Importer of Record.

This GitHub Pages preview hosts a landing page and a labeled **SAMPLE** PDF. It is **not** legal or customs advice, **not** a CBP publication, and **not** a substitute for the official Interim Final Rule. The official CBP and Federal Register text controls. The rule may be revised.
